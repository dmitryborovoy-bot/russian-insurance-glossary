📋 Sample Terms

Full machine-readable JSON: data/glossary.json

CategoryEnglish TermRussian TermDefinition
trucking MC Authority MC Authority Federal operating authority issued by FMCSA allowing motor carriers to transport regulated commodities for hire across state lines. Federal fee $300.
trucking BMC-91 BMC-91 (форма страхования) Form filed by insurance company with FMCSA proving motor carrier has minimum required liability ($750K-$5M depending on cargo).
trucking Bobtail Insurance Bobtail (страховка трака без прицепа) Coverage for a truck operating without a trailer attached, typically between dispatched loads. Average $300-$800/year.
auto SR-22 SR-22 (сертификат финансовой ответственности) Certificate filed by insurance company with state DMV proving minimum required auto insurance, typically required after DUI/DWI or license suspension.
life Final Expense Final Expense (страхование на похороны) Small whole life insurance policy ($5K-$25K) covering funeral and burial costs. Often guaranteed-issue (no medical exam) for ages 50-85.
business Workers Compensation Workers Compensation (страхование работников) Mandatory insurance covering medical expenses and lost wages for employees injured on the job. Rate $0.15-$15+ per $100 of payroll.
